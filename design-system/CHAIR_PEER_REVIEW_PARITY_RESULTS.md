## 4. Business rules checked (read only)

- Reviewers are `conference_members` (role reviewer; invited, active, declined). A paper's assignment is a `review_assignments` row; a reviewer without an account yet is queued in `pending_review_assignments` (migration 0019) and becomes an assignment when they accept. Both the new popover and the Reviewers page go through `assignPapers`, which handles that.
- Reviews are private to chairs until `set_review_shared` (view `author_reviews` for authors); decisions are recorded silently and authors learn them only after `notify_decision`. The new popover touches none of this.
- "Assign after plagiarism check": a paper can take reviewers only after screening clears (`canAssign`), as in the prototype.
- Conflict rule used by the prototype's pool: a reviewer who is an author of the paper cannot be picked; at the per-reviewer limit the reviewer is greyed out. Same here (the limit comes from `loadSettings`, `conference_settings`).

## 12.3 Assignment algorithm comparison

Rules (all from the prototype, `rankReviewersFor`, `reviewerConflicts`, `AutoAssignModal`, `newAssignmentDueISO`; keyword matching removed because the database keeps no reviewer expertise):
1. **Candidates for a paper:** the committee, minus reviewers who declined the committee, minus those already on the paper (or queued on it), minus those who declined this paper.
2. **Conflict:** a reviewer is an author (same email or same name), or shares a non-webmail email domain with an author ("Same institution as X"). The prototype's declared-institution list does not exist in the database.
3. **Load:** papers the reviewer has in this conference; **full** when load reaches the per-reviewer limit (Review settings; no limit by default).
4. **Ranking:** no conflict first, then not full, then lightest load, ties in committee order.
5. **Auto-assign plan:** papers with fewer reviewers first; each paper is filled to the target with the lightest-loaded reviewer who has no conflict and is under the limit; every pick adds to the load seen by the next paper; an empty slot stays empty when nobody qualifies.
6. **Default review deadline:** today plus "Reviewers get N days" (Review timeline), never later than the end of the reviewing period; without those days, the conference's review deadline.

Evidence: 24 unit tests (`qa/assign-rules-test.mjs`) pass: conflicts by email, name and institution, webmail ignored, duplicates, queued reviewers, declined papers, limits, plan fill / spread / order / empty slots, deadline cases. On the same fixtures the plan has the same size and the same per-reviewer totals as the prototype's. The prototype's per-paper choices differ for 24 of 35 papers: it ranks an expertise match first, and ours cannot.

**Ambiguities, documented and not guessed:**
- The prototype's Assign same reviewers does not check whether a paper can take reviewers yet (plagiarism stage, decided). Here those papers are skipped, as the single Assign and the Reviewers page already do. If you want the prototype's looser behaviour, the database's rules on assigning before the plagiarism check need a business decision.
- The prototype says one email per reviewer listing all their papers. The existing email event `reviews_assigned` is per paper, so a reviewer given three papers gets three emails; the screens say only that reviewers are emailed. Grouping needs an email-server change.
- The reviewer limit is applied in the popover and Auto-assign (greyed or never picked), not in Assign same reviewers: the prototype does not check it there either.

## 12.4 Edit decisions: workflow comparison

- Prototype list: choosing a decision (dropdown or Edit decisions) records it with `applyDecisionType`; the page's own toast says "The authors aren't emailed yet — send it from the Decided list (Notify authors)".
- Next.js: the same, through `recordDecision` (migration 0002: `record_decision` writes the chair's record only; `notify_decision` is what copies it to the author-visible `notified_*` columns and notifies). Bulk Edit decisions calls `recordDecision` once per selected paper; nothing is sent, nothing becomes author-visible, and the decision types offered are the conference's own.
- The prototype's confirmation text for a real decision says authors will be emailed and "Emails can't be unsent". That contradicts its own toast and the database, so it was not copied; the text says what happens.
- A failure on one paper does not stop the others: the toast says how many were changed and lists the ones that were not; if every paper fails the dialog stays open with the error.
- No new decision state and no new notification behaviour were added.
