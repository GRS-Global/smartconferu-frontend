Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

1. Who we are

Smartconferu ("we", "us") is a conference management platform at smartconferu.com. It helps organizers run calls for papers, peer review, programs and registration, and helps authors and reviewers take part. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), and, if you add them, your photo, affiliation, department, designation, country, gender, phone number, website and research interests.
  • Scholarly identifiers: links you choose to add, such as ORCID, Google Scholar, Scopus or DBLP.
  • Conference activity: papers and files you submit, co-author details you enter, reviews, decisions, registrations and messages sent through the Service.
  • Payment records: receipts for purchases. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Technical data: sign-in sessions, basic logs and settings stored in your browser so you stay signed in.

3. Signing in with Google or Microsoft

If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Microsoft", we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from that provider, plus a provider ID that lets you sign in again. We use them only to create and sign in to your Smartconferu account and to pre-fill your profile. We don't request access to your contacts, files, calendar, mailbox or any other data, and we never see your Google or Microsoft password.

Smartconferu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell this information, use it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone except as needed to run the Service.

4. How we use your information

  • To run your account and the conferences you take part in: submissions, review assignments, decisions, programs and registrations.
  • To show organizers and fellow committee members the details they need, for example your name and affiliation when a chair chooses reviewers.
  • To send emails about your activity, such as submission confirmations, review invitations and decision letters.
  • To provide optional AI features, such as filling in a submission form from a paper or helping reviewers write a review. These process the paper and review text, not your account details.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Smartconferu, under agreements that limit them to that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in
  • Netlify: website hosting
  • Resend: sending emails
  • Anthropic and Voyage AI: processing paper text for the optional AI features

Within a conference, organizers and committee members see the information that conference needs. We may also disclose information when required by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Papers, reviews and decisions that form part of a conference's record may be kept after you close your account, as is standard for academic record-keeping. You can ask us to delete anything else.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and update your profile at any time in My Profile. You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct or delete it, or to close your account, by writing to privacy@smartconferu.com. You can also remove Smartconferu's access from your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and access to each conference's data is restricted to the people who are part of it.

9. Children

Smartconferu is meant for researchers and conference professionals and isn't directed at children under 16.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll let you know in the app before the change applies. Questions? Contact us at privacy@smartconferu.com.
